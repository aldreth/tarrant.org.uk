SEPTEMBER 2005

My memories of Cindy by Catherine Complin x
I knew Cindy when we lived in York 12 years ago ~ we both travelled to Doncaster & taught Home Economics.
She was a wonderful person and always had so much energy & such a positive outlook on life.
I would sometimes pick her up & then we would get the train together. I particularly remember how she continued with her commitment to teaching & all the travelling, when she was heavily pregnant with Toby.
Cindy never complained & was always smiling.
I can remember you moving to Aldreth
Grove & having a lovely supper with you &
Toby at your new house. We also had a couple of walks with you in the park & fed the ducks with Toby ~ Cindy was such a calm & loving mother.
We left York in 1992 as we were posted to Germany. We have lovely memories of York & you Edward & Cindy are one of those.
I have always enjoyed keeping in touch
& receiving your news each year. I knew that Cindy was a wonderful partner & loving mother. x
