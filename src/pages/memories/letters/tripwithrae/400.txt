30 Sept 2005

Dear Edward,

I am so, so sorry to learn of Cindy's death, and truly sorry we had lost touch.
I met you briefly in OZ - not long before you and Cindy were to head home to the UK - and you were to start UNI at York. I was in OZ for Christmas and New Year
89/90, staying with my niece Karen.

The 3 of us, Cindy, Karen and me, had many happy shared memories. Our first holiday together we visited the Yorkshire Dales, Grassington, Kettlewell, Thwaite,
Keld and Hawes, and visited Bolton Abbey on the way back south. Such beautiful countryside, not far from you in York. Sadly I have no photos of that holiday.

We also had a wonderful time together visiting W. Scotland and the Isle of Skye.
I have put together a few photos for you, Toby, John and Rose.

I first met Cindy when she was a student at Guildford, she being a friend of Karen's. Saw a lot more of her when she was living and teaching in Hackney.

I always admired her, and remember thinking how organized and matter of fact she was. So mature for her years. Her willingness to have a go - her humour - always trying to see the bright side of things. Always willing to accept a challenge and support her friends.

I'm sure motherhood and family mentor gave her the challenge, fulfilment, and pleasure she desired. Very sure she was a wonderful MUM, and a dedicated and supportive wife. I'm only sorry I didn't make myself available for Cindy to share with me her pride and pleasure in her/your created family.

Such sad news - such sorrow.

My heart goes out to you.

I wish you strength. Shared love and support from one another to deal with the pain and emptiness. Cindy so dear and central to you all, taken from you far too early in your lives together.

My condolences.

Sincerely,                         and Cindy's
Rae Burbidge (Karen's auntie Rae) well actually just RAE.
