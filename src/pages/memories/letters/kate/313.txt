Monday 3rd Oct
I've been thinking about Cindy a lot today. Today's the kind of day when I will miss her.
An unspectacular trip to school and slow walk back on a grey, dull x ordinary autumn morning. I would often bump into Cindy or she'd hop off her bike along the route and we'd stop and chat for a long while, put the world to rights x wander home up Bishopthorpe Rd. On these occasions, without fail, my mood would be changed for the better by the time I reached home. We would say good-bye and say "Hey ho" in recognition that our ranting achieved little. Except it always put a smile on my face and I'll miss that lovely woman on these dull, ordinary mornings.
Kate
