SOME MEMORIES OF CINDY
Claire Gilham.

On Tuesday 6th of September 2005, Beth and I spent a lovely afternoon with Cindy.
Cindy was very chatty and funny that day, she talked a lot about how interested she was in her own medical condition, about how she wished she had seen her lung when it was removed to see exactly what was wrong. She talked also about the experiences of giving birth to Toby, John and Rose and how fascinating and amazing that had been, she took a mirror along to John's birth to get a better view of what was happening, much to the nurses surprise!

Many of my memories of Cindy are from when she and Edward would come home from their evenings out, while I was babysitting. She always sat down and talked about what they had done, we also talked a lot about the various textile projects we were working on. She was always very clear in her opinions and I came to value them very much and would talk to her about almost anything. I was so comfortable with her and she was always reassuring and wonderfully positive to talk to, especially when I had a hard first year at University.

The last image I have of Cindy is of her relaxing (with headphones round her neck) on the sofa in the downstairs front room. She was wearing a brown V-neck top with the orange rose that John had just made her pinned on her chest. She looked pleased and happy.

I can see many of Cindy's gifts in Toby, John and Rose; their confidence to be themselves and try new things, their competence in baking and making and their willingness always to find the good, and often the humour, in a situation.

Here is a funny note that Cindy left for me one babysit. I think it illustrates how thoughtful she was, and what a good sense of humour she had. It reads:

' Hello Claire
Meatballs in the fridge: (if you need some tea)
1) Balls in sauce are cooked and just need re-heating.
2) Raw balls need 20 mins. in tom. sauce.
3) John likes pork balls only.
4) Check how many balls Toby wants and you may need to cook a few raw ones extra
Pasta in end cupboard.

(You've no idea how much pleasure writing this note has given me!)

...enjoy your balls!!

J may cook if you want him to.

See you soon
Love Cindy '
