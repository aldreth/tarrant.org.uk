My Memories of Cindy

I first met Cindy when she was about 17 leaving her bike like all the others in my yard before catching the train, she always came and talked to me before going home

when Rose my daughter was getting married she said would you like me to make the bridesmaid's dresses for my little grand-daughters
Tracy & Lynn they were lovely Pale yellow with satin bows. The headdress and baskets made with real flowers later she made them Rag dolls one with Black Hair & Blue flowered dress. The other yellow hair & yellow flowered dress with lots of lace and even made little boots. We still have them and their children play with them. when she went to London she would come and tell about teaching children of many countries and later Adults to read, and the place she went to when she had the money. when she went to Australia I had letters and cards telling me all about her travels I don't know how she managed it but came home with China's bird scarer on a long pole I had to put a cover over it when the wind blew hard it made such a noise when Cindy moved to York. I thought we would lose touch
But she wrote and told me how she was getting on every time a new baby arrived.
She was Rose's first baby's god mother. (Steven is now 24)
