Dear Annette,
this is a very belated happy birthday. I have been carrying this card around a week since before your birthday and I have been inbetween places and feeling rather unwell. we have had a non-stop holiday so far and the children have been enjoying themselves. We started with a week in Keswick in the Lake District. Edward took them all scrambling and on walks of modest length as Rose is still a reluctant walker. Toby bought himself a dinghy which proved great fun on the lake. We came home for a few days and then stayed with Mandy and family to take part in Dad's 70th birthday treat, a few rounds of Thruxton race course in a various fast cars. He was like the cat that got the cream and we all felt we'd bought him exactly the right thing. From there we went to Snowdonia to stay with Edward's family in a lovely house with a fantastic view. There were 16 of us in all including six children.
The grounds included access to a small waterfall that plunged into a pool that proved just right for diving and swimming. John + Rose camped in the garden for a couple of nights. They pitched the tent as far from the house as they could and I was sure they wouldn't last, but they had a wonderful time and enjoyed the excitement of being 'alone'! I particularly enjoyed a rail journey with Lance (E's father) and the children on the Ffestiniog railway. we have now been back home just over a week. Toby went away for a week with the YHA on a watersports week. He is particularly keen on sailing at the moment - his friend
