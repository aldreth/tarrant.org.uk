Some memories ---

Cindy leaving Heathrow airport to go on her trip to Australia -- where she then met you Edward! I went to see her off -- and felt as excited as she did about the adventure ahead, (the photo is from her trip - see her writing on reverse)!
Visiting Bosham and staying with Pat and Maurice. For many years afterwards,
Pat used to send me all her Christmas cards to use in school - and I was very grateful!

Enjoying many bottles of Sainsbury
Hungarian Merlot in Cindy's flat in Newsham.

Dear Edward, Toby, John and Rose

Well, I am not sure how to begin this card as there are so many words I want to use to describe how wonderful Cindy was. Warm, respectful, a lover of life and a
"liver" in the moment, a fount of good sense and wisdom, a wonderful sense of humour and of irony, a woman with class, style and wonderful good taste. (Although I can't remember the name, Cindy did advise me to buy a particular brand of saucepan and just to always have the best you can afford ... advice I will carry always)!
And Cindy also had a wonderful husband and three fantastic children - I am thinking of you all. Love Jane xxx
