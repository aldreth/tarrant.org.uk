I have many scattered memories of Cindy, mainly from when we lived on Bishopthorpe Road, & up until
Louis and Toby were about 8 years old. I remember cosy New Year's Eves playing Pictionary, and big family suppers with, of course, wonderful food. I remember taking all the kids down to Rowntree Park with her, and her astute, amusing comments about our own and other peoples kids... Cindy was always observant, always interested, and so hospitable, a great 'provider' — I remember a party you gave, when you'd been to a pick-your-own strawberry farm, and there was this great bowl of strawberries on the table — it typified her somehow.
Cindy once did me the kindness of giving me a personal reference for a job application I was doing. She was businesslike and organised as we got the details of the post from me... and then she went away and came up with a few comments that were so to the point, and thoughtful - she told me something I had not previously known about myself — helped me get the job I am sure (!) and helped me since, just having an aspect of myself
