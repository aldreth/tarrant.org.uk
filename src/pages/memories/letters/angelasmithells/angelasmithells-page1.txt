Dear Edward
Having never attended a quaker meeting before, I felt very moved last Sunday.
It was very lovely to share in all the wonderful memories. If I'd had a little more courage I would like to have said something myself - I would like to have spoken as a parent of one of Cindy
(Ms Tarrant's) pupils and form tutees

I remember clearly speaking to Cindy at Joanna's first parents' evening at Abbey Grange
She remarked that Joanna was quite reserved, which was true, but as Joanna had always been discarded at her junior school as a
(quiet child)! I was very keen to prevent any such label being attached at High School
So I immediately replied that Joanna may have seemed quieter, but actually possessed a deep inner confidence
When I received Joanna's report later in the year, I was touched to see that Cindy had remembered my words and written them on Joanna's report
I felt that from that time
