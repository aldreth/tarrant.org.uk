Dear Edward

We were so sad to hear of Cindy's death — her courage and positive attitude have been an inspiration to many of us. I always enjoyed my occasional visits to you, because of your warmth and humour.

I grieve for you and the children but feel that you and Cindy between you have given them one of the best starts in life which will stand them in good stead in the difficult months ahead and throughout their life.
