Christmas 2005

Dear Edward
I only met Cindy a handful of times but on
each occasion was struck by her strength,
her matter-of-fact enjoyment of life and
her love of her family.
The last couple of times I saw her were at
Carol's where we were knitting - it was a
pleasure to be in her company and I shall
treasure the memory.
We send you all our love : Christmas is a hard
time for memories , but I am sure you will
all have many happy ones of lovely family occasions
together. The forging of new traditions alongside
the familiar is very restoring in the end.
Perhaps we'll see you in the New Year - of course,
if there were ever any way we could offer any
help , we'd be glad to.

Much love Annabel.
