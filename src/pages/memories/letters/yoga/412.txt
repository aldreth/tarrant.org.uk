I can't remember how many years Cindy has been part of our Yoga group - but I remember the first night she came. She told me about her illness + that she had only one lung. Since Yoga puts such emphasis on breathing I left that night thinking "well this could be a bit of a challenge!"
But it was not a challenge - it was a delight.

Two special memories to share - one "tarrattasana" while the rest of us were falling over Cindy looked serene and balanced. The other is Cindy's shower posture "vrkasana" (the tree). I will miss her smile, her laughter and her fingers massaging my shoulders. I am so pleased Cindy came into my life. Right now, like many others, I am sad she has gone, I miss her presence but not sorry her suffering is over. I feel her looking down on us and smiling. Our thoughts are with her spirit and with Edward, Toby
Rose + John. Om Shanti Peace
