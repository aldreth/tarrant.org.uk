September 12 '05

Dear Edward

Whilst, in more recent times, Cindy's death has been expected, we hoped that somehow she would survive her last illness. The news of her dying has therefore still come as a great shock and our deepest sympathy goes out to you, the children & Cindy's family.
Cindy was a courageous woman, in great adversity, which we admired and wondered at. She always brought more than a few rays of sunshine + cheer into a room when we met her and was always great fun to be with. She surely will be missed by all who knew her.

Our thoughts are now with you, Toby, John and Rose and if there is any help we can give you in this sad time, please let us know.

With condolences.

Love,
Anne + Peter
