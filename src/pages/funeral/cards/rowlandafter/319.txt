Scotland, Monday 26th

Dear Edward
I just wanted to say hello, and that we are thinking of you all I would have liked to have spoken to you on Sunday but I was so distressed to say anything sensible, and you had enough to contend with anyway
If there is anything we can do, you only have to say. Perhaps when you feel able, you might like to come to Oxford with the children, or perhaps they would like to come for a few days by themselves. I still love my own rifle, and Janet teaches at the dry ski slope, so we could find something for them to do. They were so brave on Sunday

Goodbye for now Edward
Rowland
