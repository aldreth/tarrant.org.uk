Miss J M Cobb
ASSOCIATION FOR INTERNATIONAL CANCER RESEARCH

17th Sept. '05

Dear Edward,
I was very sorry to learn this morning of Cindy's death.
It came as a complete shock as my last news had been that she was back working.

Sadly I will be unable to attend the funeral as within a few hours I am leaving for Australia, staying with Pip & Colin.
However, my thoughts are with you and the children. It is a difficult time for you all but I am sure Cindy's fortitude shone through to the end.

May God be with you all.
Sincerely,
Joyce
