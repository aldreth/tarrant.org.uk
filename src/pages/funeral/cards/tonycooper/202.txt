19 September 2005

Dear Edward and family

I was so sorry to hear the news of
Cindy's death. Although I knew it was not
unexpected, I hoped it was news I'd never
hear. And though it may have been inevitable,
it does not diminish the event when it
eventually occurs.

I cannot claim to have known Cindy
well, but it was always a pleasure to
meet her; she was so easy-going and
comfortable to be with. It was clear to
me that she was a very special person,
a bright spark in an often gloomy world.
It is encouraging to think that some of
that brightness will live on in your family.
Naomi, Ben and Hanna were saddened
to hear of Cindy's death, and they join me
in sending our deepest sympathy to you and
the children. I hope that as well as
mourning Cindy's death, you will be able to
celebrate her life too.

Yours, with the sympathy of us all,
Tony Cooper.
