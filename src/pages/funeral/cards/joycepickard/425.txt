18.09.05.

Dear Edward,
You and the children were very much in the Friends
and love of the Meeting this morning. It was strange
that in the same gathering we welcomed a new baby,
(Jennifer, born to Jane Edwards and Peter Ashton) and
learnt of the end of Cindy's long struggle. We were
very much aware of the love which holds families
together, and the quiet yet central role of the mother.
We remembered Cindy's courage and the way she faced
her illness: her clear thought, her preparedness, and
her determination to make the transition, and the
acceptance, as easy as possible for you and the
children. But your loss is enormous, and irreparable.
May your courage and strength help you in those
difficult days, and your loss enable you to support
and comfort the children in their loss! Every blessing,
Joyce Pickard
