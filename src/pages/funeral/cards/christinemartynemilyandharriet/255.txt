Thursday 15th Sept

Dear Edward,
I learned the sad news of Cindy's death
yesterday. I send you my love and sympathy.
Cindy was a beautiful wise woman and the
love you two shared was a joy to behold. For
you and the children there must be a terrible
numbness but I hope that you will be able
to smile again as you remember happy times
shared together.

We are very sorry that the weekend of the
24th September is our Duke of Edinburgh training in
the Yorkshire Dales - but we will be with you all
in spirit as you say farewell to Cindy.
Love and prayers to you all, Christine, Martyn, Emily & Harriet
