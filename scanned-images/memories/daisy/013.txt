Dear Cindy.
Was so kind, thoughtful,
and sincere. Cindy was
so lovely, and had a smile
and Cheerful word for
every-one. In all she had to
bear with her health. Her
courage, was an inspiration to us
all.

With Deepest Sympathy

To. Edward. Toby, John, & Rose.
Cindy. Loved Edward, and the
Children, and all her Family.
Cindy in return was Loved by
everyone. We shall all miss
Cindy. But remember her
lovely smiling face. And
follow her example.
Cindy is resting peacefully
now. And watching over us all.
And wanting us to be happy in
The Future. God Bless her.
With my Love. From. Daisy. x
