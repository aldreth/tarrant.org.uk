On our way home
stayed night at
BEN NEVIS YHA.
Nice sunny afternoon
so decided to climb
Ben Nevis - took 3hrs.
to climb and only 1 hr.
to descend.

Well worn path to walk + scramble up.
Didn't quite make the top as a
blizzard started. Snow came down
thick and fast. Totally unexpected!!
Good job we were all 3 wearing
bright colours as visibility
became almost zero.

Some walkers were
only wearing T'Shirts,
it had been such a
sunny afternoon when
we started.
As we descended the
snow became cold, cold
Rain - and this was
only beginning of September!!.
