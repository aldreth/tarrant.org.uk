Cindy to the Rescue

Oh no! It was Nok's dance show in a few
weeks time, and we were supposed to be making
costumes... But neither Jenny nor I can sew.
I didn't even try, knowing it would be a disaster.
Jenny attempted to adjust a Greek tunic ~ cutting
it while it was pinned to Nok, resulting in a
strange S-shaped rag, beyond repair. However
would we manage the Siamese costume, which
needed to be made from scratch?!

A couple of evenings later, we went to a
multicultural evening at the Priory Centre in
York. Cindy, Rose and John were there. While
the kids went off to get henna tattoos and
do Indian stick dancing, the mums sat around
chatting ~ about teaching mostly (no wonder Nok,
Rose and John kept themselves busy!) Cindy was
telling us about her job as a textiles teacher, about
her G.C.S.E students who wanted to "do" fashion,
about saying goodbye to them when she became
ill, and about how much she was enjoying
spending time with Rose, John, Toby and Edward
now she was at home. But the topic of textiles
inevitably led to our sewing problems. Five minutes
into our description of our attempts, Cindy cried
"No more! I'll make them!" She came around a
couple of days later. I'd pinned the pattern to the
material. "Shall I cut it out?" I asked. "No!" She
replied, quickly rescuing the fabric.
