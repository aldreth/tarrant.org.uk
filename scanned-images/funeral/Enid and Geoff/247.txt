15. ix. 05.

Dear Edward,

Jan Walker has given me the sad news
about Cindy's death. I am so very
sorry.

This must be a very difficult time for
you, but I hope that in due course you
will get some consolation from the memories
of all the happy times you shared, and be
thankful that her suffering is at an end.

Words seem inadequate, but we do feel
for you and are thinking about you all.

With love.
Enid
